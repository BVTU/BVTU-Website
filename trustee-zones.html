Zone boundaries follow Policy 1.100 (Trustee Representation), amended 23 February 2022, drawn against surveyed parcel, road and river data from the BC Data Catalogue. The policy's "Eckman Road" is locally the same road as Babine Lake Road. Boundaries are accurate to within a few hundred metres and are shown for orientation. If you live close to a line, confirm your zone with the district office before voting.
